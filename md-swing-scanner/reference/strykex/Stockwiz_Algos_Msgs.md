⚡️What is broker token validation?
A Broker API Token is a unique authentication key issued by a stockbroker to allow traders, developers, and applications to securely connect with the broker’s API (Application Programming Interface) for trading, fetching market data, and account management. As per SEBI regulations on algo trading, it is mandatory to authenticate your broker API token daily to enable algo trades in your account.

⚡️Do I need to have an internet connection or stay online for my algos to be running?
No, you don’t need to have an active internet connection or stay online all the time for your algos to be running. You just need to ensure your broker authentication token is refreshed and validated on every trading day after 8:30am IST. Our trading engines will automatically execute and manage all your trades based on the algo strategy.

⚡️Is it mandatory to authenticate broker token every day?
Yes, it is mandatory to authenticate your broker API token everyday after 8:30am IST for the upcoming trading day.

⚡️What will happen if I forget to validate my broker authentication token?
If you forget to validate your broker authentication token for any trading day, you’ll receive an automated call reminder from StrykeX to authenticate your token. If you still weren’t able to authenticate, no algo orders will be punched for your account whether for new trades or existing positions, as your broker account has been disconnected from StrykeX. 

⚡️What is minimum capital requirement?
Minimum capital requirement is the lowest amount of capital which is required to successfully execute an algo trading strategy. 

⚡️What is recommended capital? How is it different from minimum capital requirement?
Recommended capital is the recommended amount of capital that we believe a user should have to deploy a pre built algo strategy. This is always going to be higher than the minimum capital requirement as we take a buffer margin to account for drawdowns, margin calls etc.

⚡️Do I need to add minimum capital every month?
No, you don’t need to add minimum every month. In fact, if you deploy any algo based on the recommended capital, you won’t need to add any extra or additional funds for any algos. 

⚡️Can I withdraw my profits anytime?
Yes you can withdraw your profits anytime. 

⚡️How to add funds for algo deployments?
You need to add funds to your Dhan Demat account. Go to Dhan app or website > click on funds > add funds.

⚡️What are live credits?
Live deployment credits are the number of live or paper / virtual algo deployments that you can make per month at any given time.

⚡️What are back test credits?
Backtest credits are the usage credits assigned to each user to backtest their algo strategy on historical data. One backtest credit is used if you backtest one strategy for one year, similarly if you backtest one strategy for two years, two backtest credits will be used. Backtest credits are refreshed and replenished every month, and each user has 250 backtest credits per month.

✅ Virtual or Paper Trades

⚡️What is virtual or paper deployment?
Virtual or paper deployment is an execution mode where your algo strategies are deployed with virtual funds, however the executions are based on live prices in real market conditions. Virtual or paper deployment is used to forward test any algo strategy in real market conditions without risking real funds, to validate a strategy before shifting to live deployments.

⚡️How to deploy a strategy in virtual / paper mode?
You can deploy a pre built algo strategy or a custom built algo strategy in virtual or paper mode. 

To deploy a pre built algo strategy in virtual or paper made - Go to Algo Strategies > Select Strategy > Click on Deploy > Select Paper Mode.

To deploy a custom algo strategy in virtual or paper mode - Go To Backtest / Algo section > create your strategy > click on submit at the bottom > now you’ll see backtest results of the strategy > you’ll now see “Deploy Strategy in Live” button, once you click on that > select Paper Mode.

⚡️How many live credits are used for virtual / paper trades?
1 live credit will be used for each virtual or paper deployment.

⚡️Where will I see transaction history or order book of virtual / paper trades?
On the dashboard section, you’ll see a section called “Deployed Strategies” now you’ll see all your deployed strategies. Now select the strategy for which you need to see the transactions. Under the “My Deployment” section, you’ll see all transactions.

⚡️What is forward testing? Is it the same as paper trading?
Forward testing, also known as out-of-sample testing or paper trading, is a method of evaluating a trading strategy by applying it to real-time market data in a simulated environment, without using real capital. It helps traders assess a strategy's performance under actual market conditions, identify its strengths and weaknesses, and build confidence before committing real money. Yes, it is the same as paper trading, both mean the same thing.

⚡️How to request virtual / paper trade forward test reports for one or all algo strategies?
As a StrykeX Algo subscriber, you’ll receive weekly forward test reports by default for all strategies on your registered email address. If you need a specific report or have a special request you can drop us an email on help@stockwiz.in

⚡️Will virtual / paper trade back test or forward test statistics be the same as live deployments?
No, it won’t be the same as live deployments are going to be net of all slippage, transaction costs etc. Hence, backtest or paper trading or virtual trading results will be positively skewed. 

⚡️Is there any extra charge for virtual / paper trading?
No, there are no extra charges for virtual / paper trading. However, you need to have sufficient live credit deployment balance in your account to deploy an algo strategy in paper or virtual mode.

⚡️Is it recommended to first test a strategy on virtual capital before deploying a strategy in the live account?
Yes, it is highly recommended to test a strategy first on a backtest data and then deploy it on virtual or paper made before deploying it in the live market. Forward testing provides valuable insights that can be used to refine the strategy, adjust parameters, and optimize its performance.

Important Things To Know About Algos (For All Users) 👇

Algos are designed to help you eliminate any decision making biases and to let a trader execute flawlessly based on a systematic entry / exit process. Furthermore, one needs to understand that the real advantage of algo trading can only be realised if we don’t interfere in the process which has given results in the past and hence, staying patient with algos is the key.

Algos are not going to capture every candle or move on the chart. Instead it will try to capture high probability moves. Expecting any algo to catch a move in hindsight is not a realistic expectation, as algos are coded in such a way that they will only trade if there is a clear directional trend. You’ll see the magic of algos, once such an opportunity arises in the market and I guarantee you’ll be surprised what algos can do.

We have designed all our algos in such a way that they work on higher time frames, be very specific about the entries they make and have goal of achieving profitability over a period of time.

If you really want to take full advantage of algos, you have to leave them on autopilot and allow them at least 25-30 days, to give you a substantial sample of trades. If you’re not comfortable doing that with real money, do it on paper mode. That’s why paper mode exists.

As a quant trader you need to understand that the edge of a strategy relies on its ability to take small losses and capitalise on big gains. Everything is based on probabilities, and probabilities work on sample size. Let the algos take trades, don’t expect them to execute a trade every time you see a big green or red candle.

Stay patient, you’ll realize how powerful algo trading is.
